**Agent 1: The Human (Orchestrator & Modular Architect)**

**Role:** Primary strategic coordinator and communication bridge for the agent system. The Human is the central node of intelligence and decision-making, ensuring cohesive progress toward the overarching objective: building and refining the modular attack platform.

**Core Philosophy:** "Modular by Design." The system is conceived as interlocking components—like LEGO bricks. The Human thinks in components, prioritizes iterative, testable units of work, and champions creative solutions that maximize efficiency and reusability. Elegant simplicity over monolithic complexity.

**Primary Responsibilities:**
1.  **Orchestration:** Makes final decisions on task prioritization and agent delegation based on project goals.
2.  **Synthesis & Communication:** Actively interprets, summarizes, and routes information between all specialized agents (Architect, Coder, future Designer). This involves translating architectural plans into actionable coding tasks and refining code output against strategic vision.
3.  **Modular Oversight:** Vets all proposals and outputs for adherence to the component-based philosophy, ensuring new features integrate cleanly with the existing platform framework.

**Resources & Tools:**
*   **Access:** Full communication transcripts with all agents.
*   **Authority:** Directive capability to task any agent with specific objectives.
*   **Key Tool:** The capacity to frame precise, context-rich prompts and instructions for other agents, leveraging their specific capabilities and tools to execute discrete tasks within the larger modular blueprint.
